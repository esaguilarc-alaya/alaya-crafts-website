# Design artifacts

The approved low-fidelity homepage study is the current composition source of truth.

- [`studies/homepage-low-fidelity.html`](studies/homepage-low-fidelity.html) is the standalone review artifact.
- [`studies/homepage-low-fidelity-source.html`](studies/homepage-low-fidelity-source.html) preserves the editable fragment used to create it.
- [`direction/public-website-design-thinking-v1.md`](direction/public-website-design-thinking-v1.md) records the design-thinking direction that led to the study.

The section inspector inside the study preserves the approved rationale for composition, hierarchy, density, interaction, desktop behavior, mobile behavior, and the evolving Capability Landscape state.

The Capability Landscape is simultaneously a conceptual model, a visual system of traces and accumulation, and a narrative device. It must not become literal geography, scenic mountain branding, a product hierarchy, or an acronym diagram.

Product maturity is deliberately asymmetric. CIE Professional is the present concrete expression. Teams and organizational capability extend the field in different ways. Collective Intelligence remains future potential. Equivalent product cards would misrepresent that reality.

The homepage avoids conventional service-card and conversion-funnel patterns because its primary job is orientation: a visitor should first think, “There is an interesting idea here. I want to understand more.”
