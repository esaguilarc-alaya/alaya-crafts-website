# Slice 03 — Ideas: Resolved Decisions (2026-08-27)

**Status:** Governing decisions for the Ideas low-fidelity studies. Read alongside `03-ideas.md`.

## Resolved decisions

- Keep **Ideas** as the public label.
- Publish all three maturity states—**Developed**, **Emerging**, and **Exploratory**—as visible, plain-language metadata.
- Use **Alaya Crafts**, in the third person, as the authorial voice.
- Keep V1 publishing file-based. No CMS is required for this slice.
- “Follow the Ideas” means returning to the index or following Alaya through an existing public channel. It does not imply a newsletter, RSS feed, or publication cadence.

## Recommended V1 content model

The design supports three formats without requiring all three to be populated at launch:

- **Essays** — developed arguments or points of view.
- **Notes** — shorter observations, questions, or ideas still taking shape.
- **Field Work** — practices, methods, or lessons grounded in actual work where publishable.

One genuinely strong piece is enough to anchor launch. The index should remain comfortable with being short.

## Placeholder content guardrail

Until real launchable material is selected, every study item must use an unmistakable “Qwerty” placeholder title. Placeholder material must never be mistaken for a real Alaya publication.

## Index structure

1. Restrained opening: Ideas as another way Alaya explores the capability landscape.
2. One featured current inquiry.
3. The available ways of reading, treated as formats rather than filter controls.
4. A quiet chronological body-of-thought index with date, category, and maturity state.
5. Contextual connections only where a genuine relationship exists.
6. An open ending without a fabricated capture mechanism.

## Reading-template requirements

- Comfortable long-form reading measure and heading hierarchy.
- Category, date, and maturity state visible near the title.
- Support for block quotations and lightweight references.
- Contextual links to CIE Professional or For Organizations only where relevant.
- No related-content carousel, comments, share controls, progress bar, or estimated-reading-time badge.

## Still excluded

Empty category pages, invented articles, fabricated cadence or subscriber counts, CMS/search/tagging/recommendations/comments/accounts, lead-gated downloads, SEO-content-factory behavior, and presenting exploratory notes as settled doctrine.
