# Slice 02 — For Organizations Public Page

**Status:** Ready after Slice 01 low-fidelity approval  
**Type:** Organizational orientation page, not a consulting-services catalogue

## Primary visitor question

How does Alaya's capability philosophy help an organization understand what it can already do, what prevents strategy from becoming coherent work, and what it may need to cultivate next?

## Slice responsibility

Create an honest organizational doorway into Alaya without reducing the company to consulting services or implying that CIE Organization is already a mature product.

The practical entry remains:

> We help organizations reduce friction between strategy and execution.

The page must reveal that this friction is often connected to capability, knowledge, systems, relationships, and the way work moves—not merely process inefficiency.

## Intended visitor understanding

By the end of the page, a visitor should understand:

- capability already exists across people, teams, systems, knowledge, and work;
- organizations often struggle to see, connect, preserve, and cultivate it;
- technology alone does not create the capability needed to sustain change;
- Alaya examines the conditions beneath execution rather than selling a standard transformation package;
- organizational work is real today, while productized team or organization expressions remain emerging unless explicitly confirmed otherwise.

## Working narrative hypothesis

1. **Opening:** Capability connects strategy to the work that carries it.
2. **The organizational condition:** strategy loses coherence as it moves through roles, decisions, systems, incentives, knowledge, and execution.
3. **What is difficult to see:** important capability may be fragmented, implicit, duplicated, constrained, or unable to travel.
4. **A different lens:** understanding capability changes what an organization can pursue, draw upon, and cultivate.
5. **Questions Alaya explores:** use real organizational questions rather than service pillars.
6. **The wider landscape:** individual, team, organizational, knowledge, and workflow relationships become visible at different scales.
7. **How Alaya currently works:** describe only confirmed engagement forms when available.
8. **Technology's role:** connect and extend understanding; never become the protagonist.
9. **Human provenance:** the approach emerged through sustained work across technology, engineering, leadership, product, systems, knowledge, and execution.
10. **Action:** a quiet, direct conversation with a person.

## Organizational questions to test

- Where does strategy lose coherence as it becomes work?
- What knowledge disappears between teams or systems?
- Where is technology introduced without the capability needed to sustain it?
- What capability already exists but remains fragmented, implicit, or difficult to reuse?
- What relationships between capability, work, knowledge, and decisions are difficult to see?

These remain working territories, not a disguised service catalogue.

## Composition direction

- A widening field: the individual is no longer the only unit of understanding.
- Use relationships, conditions, and questions rather than equal service cards.
- Maintain editorial restraint and substantial negative space.
- Asymmetry should communicate that current organizational work and emerging product directions are not equivalent.
- Avoid boardroom imagery, consulting diagrams, transformation funnels, capability heatmaps, and maturity scores.

## Required design decisions

- What two or three engagement forms genuinely exist today?
- Is “We help organizations reduce friction between strategy and execution” supporting language, a headline, or a secondary practical anchor?
- How should teams appear without implying a finished CIE Teams product?
- What organizational examples can be discussed without customer evidence or confidentiality concerns?
- What is the direct Contact mechanism and expected response promise?

## Deliverables

1. Short design-thinking interpretation.
2. Page narrative and minimum section structure.
3. Working copy organized around conditions and questions.
4. Low-fidelity desktop and mobile composition.
5. Interaction and scale-change notes.
6. Critical review against consulting-theater risks.
7. Explicit list of engagement, maturity, and evidence claims requiring confirmation.

## Excluded from this slice

- Inventing a service catalogue, packages, pricing, case studies, logos, testimonials, or outcomes.
- Presenting CIE Teams or CIE Organization as available products without confirmation.
- Organizational assessment tools, surveys, diagnostic forms, or lead qualification.
- Calendly-first composition, chatbot, AI intake, or conversion funnel.
- Detailed MCI or Collective Intelligence product claims.

## Acceptance criteria

- Organizational work feels like an expression of Alaya's capability philosophy, not an attached consultancy.
- A visitor can recognize practical organizational relevance without encountering generic service pillars.
- Current work and future product potential remain visibly distinct.
- The page produces thoughtful questions rather than unsupported certainty.
- Contact is direct, human, and proportionate.

## Stop condition

Stop after the responsive low-fidelity page study and critical review. Do not begin polished design or production implementation until reviewed.
