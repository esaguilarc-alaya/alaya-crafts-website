# Slice 03 — Ideas Public Page

**Status:** Ready after the product and organizational narratives are stable  
**Type:** Body-of-thinking index and reading experience, not a generic Insights feed

## Primary visitor question

What is Alaya learning and developing, and how can I explore the ideas without first becoming a customer or product user?

## Slice responsibility

Define a credible public home for Alaya's evolving body of thought.

The page should make curiosity itself a valid form of participation. It must not exist merely to demonstrate thought leadership, generate leads, or imitate a corporate content hub.

## Intended visitor understanding

By the end of the page, a visitor should understand:

- Alaya's ideas develop through practice, observation, technology, design, and reflection;
- capability, knowledge, technology, crafts, professional understanding, and organizational work are connected fields of inquiry;
- published material has different maturity states and should not all be presented as settled doctrine;
- reading can lead naturally toward CIE Professional, organizational work, or further ideas without becoming a conversion mechanism.

## Minimum viable content model

Do not launch this page until at least one real item exists.

Start with a deliberately small model:

- **Essays** — developed arguments or points of view.
- **Notes** — shorter observations, questions, or ideas still taking shape.
- **Field work** — practices, methods, or lessons grounded in actual work, only where appropriate and publishable.

These labels are working hypotheses. Use fewer if the real initial corpus does not justify all three.

## Working narrative hypothesis

1. **Opening:** Ideas are another way Alaya explores the capability landscape.
2. **Current inquiry:** one featured real piece or question, not a generic hero claim.
3. **Ways of reading:** a restrained set of real formats or themes.
4. **The body of thought:** chronological or editorially curated items with visible maturity and date.
5. **Connections:** related product, organizational, or philosophical pathways appear contextually.
6. **Open end:** follow the ideas only if a real publication/subscription mechanism exists; otherwise offer navigation without a fake newsletter promise.

## Composition direction

- Thoughtful publication or research notebook rather than blog-card grid.
- Use typography, marginalia, dates, type, and reading time sparingly.
- Let the Capability Landscape appear through connections among ideas, not decorative contours behind every article.
- Prefer one strong featured item and a quiet index over a wall of equal cards.
- Article-reading design must prioritize comfortable measure, hierarchy, citations or references where needed, and accessible navigation.

## Required design decisions

- What real material exists for V1?
- Is “Ideas” the best public label, or should it eventually become Notes, Journal, Field Notes, or another Alaya-specific term?
- Which content states are public: developed, emerging, exploratory?
- Who is the authorial voice: Alaya Crafts, named individuals, or both?
- Will publishing be file-based initially, or is a CMS genuinely needed?
- Does “Follow the Ideas” lead to email, RSS, social channels, or simply the index?

## Deliverables

1. Inventory of real launchable material and gaps.
2. Content model recommendation based on that inventory.
3. Index-page narrative and minimum structure.
4. One article/essay reading-template study.
5. Low-fidelity desktop and mobile compositions for index and reading view.
6. Metadata and connection behavior.
7. Critical review against generic content-marketing patterns.

## Excluded from this slice

- Empty category pages or invented articles.
- Fabricated publication cadence, newsletter list, subscriber count, or social proof.
- Full CMS implementation, search, tagging system, recommendations engine, comments, accounts, or personalization.
- SEO content factory behavior or lead-gated downloads.
- Treating exploratory notes as settled Alaya doctrine.

## Acceptance criteria

- The page contains or is explicitly waiting for real publishable material.
- It feels like an evolving body of knowledge rather than a marketing blog.
- Content maturity is honest and understandable.
- Reading remains valuable without a conversion action.
- Connections to CIE Professional and organizational work are contextual rather than promotional.

## Stop condition

Stop after the content-model recommendation, responsive low-fidelity index and reading studies, and critical review. Do not begin polished design, CMS work, or production implementation until reviewed.
