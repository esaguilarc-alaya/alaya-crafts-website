# Slice 01 — CIE Professional Public Page

**Status:** Ready for design-thinking and low-fidelity composition  
**Type:** Product-story page, not product application UI

## Primary visitor question

What is CIE Professional, what understanding does it help a person build, and why is that meaningfully different from a résumé builder, career platform, professional profile, or AI career tool?

## Slice responsibility

Create the public doorway into CIE Professional as the first concrete product expression of Alaya's capability philosophy.

The page must make the relationship legible:

- **Capability Intelligence** is the idea of making capability understandable through the knowledge, evidence, experience, objectives, and relationships that give it shape.
- The **Capability Intelligence Engine (CIE)** is the technology Alaya is building to make that understanding usable.
- **CIE Professional** is the current expression for an individual professional.

## Intended visitor understanding

By the end of the page, a visitor should understand that CIE Professional:

- develops an evidence-grounded professional understanding over time;
- begins with curiosity and conversation rather than profile completion;
- connects experience, evidence, capability, objectives, reflection, and representation;
- preserves uncertainty and distinguishes what is known from what is still emerging;
- helps a person understand, cultivate, and express capability;
- is not a score, static profile, résumé generator, job tracker, or omniscient AI assessment.

## Working narrative hypothesis

1. **Opening:** Understand the capability your work has cultivated.
2. **The limitation of conventional representation:** professional life is richer than titles, skill lists, and a current résumé.
3. **What CIE builds:** a professional understanding grounded in evidence, experience, reflection, objectives, and contribution.
4. **How it begins:** bring a document, connect an existing representation, or simply start a conversation.
5. **How understanding develops:** curiosity → understanding → learning → contribution → reflection.
6. **What remains connected:** experiences, evidence, capability, objectives, identity, representations, and contextual work.
7. **Epistemic character:** established, emerging, unknown, and revisable understanding remain distinct.
8. **The professional landscape:** the product helps someone explore their own capability landscape from within it.
9. **Current availability/action:** use only the real access or waitlist state confirmed at the time of design.
10. **Return to Alaya:** CIE Professional is one expression within the broader Alaya capability landscape.

## Composition direction

- Calm editorial product story with occasional inspectable glimpses of real CIE language.
- No dashboard screenshot wall and no application-shell simulation.
- Use progressive disclosure to reveal depth without turning the page into product documentation.
- The Capability Landscape can become more personal and evidence-aware here, but must not reproduce the product's Today screen literally.
- Show example language only when clearly marked as illustrative and faithful to existing product semantics.

## Required design decisions

- What is the honest V1 action: enter, request access, follow development, or another confirmed path?
- How much product UI can be shown without creating a fake experience?
- Which CIE concepts require public explanation and which remain product-internal?
- How should privacy, ownership, evidence, and epistemic restraint be communicated without legal or technical overload?
- What wording differentiates “professional understanding” from “professional profile” most clearly?

## Deliverables

1. Short design-thinking interpretation.
2. Page narrative and minimum section structure.
3. Working copy.
4. Low-fidelity desktop and mobile composition.
5. Interaction notes.
6. Critical review against the visitor question.
7. Explicit list of product claims or maturity statements requiring confirmation.

## Excluded from this slice

- CIE product implementation or application UI changes.
- Authentication, onboarding, pricing, account creation, waitlist mechanics, or payment.
- Invented features, screenshots, testimonials, metrics, or availability claims.
- Detailed architecture, governance, knowledge-model, or API explanations.
- Teams, Organizations, or Collective Intelligence beyond concise relationship context.

## Acceptance criteria

- A newcomer can explain CIE Professional without calling it an AI résumé tool.
- The page feels unmistakably related to Alaya and CIE without resembling a SaaS feature page.
- Evidence and epistemic humility are perceptible in the design and language.
- The action offered matches real product maturity.
- Mobile composition remains calm and understandable.

## Stop condition

Stop after the responsive low-fidelity page study and critical review. Do not begin polished design or production implementation until reviewed.
