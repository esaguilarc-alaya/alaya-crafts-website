# Slice 04 — Contact Public Page

**Status:** Direction resolved; polished study ready for review
**Type:** Compact direct-contact page

## Page responsibility

Give every public invitation across the Alaya Crafts website one clear, human destination.

The page is not an intake funnel, qualification mechanism, consulting-services prompt, or automated support surface. It should make starting a conversation feel possible without turning curiosity into a sales transaction.

## Approved content

### Headline

Start a conversation.

### Invitation

Alaya is an evolving body of work. If something here connects with a question you're exploring, an idea you're developing, or work you're trying to understand, you can reach us directly.

### Contact path

**Write to Alaya**  
[curious@alayacrafts.com](mailto:curious@alayacrafts.com)

Your message will be read and answered by a person.

## Design direction

- One compact page in the established Alaya logo, typography, olive/ivory palette, header, and footer system.
- The email address is the only contact mechanism.
- Maintain the same real responsive breakpoint and mobile Explore navigation used by the other public studies.
- Give the direct address sufficient prominence without styling it as a high-pressure conversion button.

## Excluded

- Contact or qualification form
- Chatbot or AI intermediary
- Calendly or schedule-a-call-first composition
- Topic, company-size, budget, or urgency fields
- Newsletter capture
- Response-time promise
- Separate contributor-submission workflow

## Acceptance criteria

- A visitor can understand why and how to contact Alaya immediately.
- The public email address is visible and usable as a direct email link.
- The page explicitly says the message will be read and answered by a person.
- The page remains compact and coherent at desktop and mobile widths.
- No additional contact pathway or consulting-theater pattern is introduced.
