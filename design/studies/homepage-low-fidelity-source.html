06 — Where the idea is taking form

Capability Intelligence Engine (CIE)

A way of making capability understandable through the knowledge, evidence, experiences, and relationships that give it shape. When capability becomes visible, its relationships can become understandable.

Alaya calls this Capability Intelligence: understanding capability through the knowledge, evidence, experience, objectives, and relationships that surround it.

The Capability Intelligence Engine (CIE) is the technology Alaya is building to make that understanding usable.

One landscape. Different scales and ways of understanding it.
