# Alaya Crafts Website

This repository owns the public website for **Alaya Crafts**.

Alaya Crafts is an emerging ecosystem organized around a central point of view:

> Capability is the enduring asset.  
> Knowledge preserves it.  
> Technology extends it.

The website introduces that idea, the Capability Landscape, and the different ways Alaya is beginning to give the idea form. It is intentionally structured as a conceptual journey rather than a conventional consulting-company landing page or a software acquisition funnel.

## Ownership boundary

The website represents Alaya Crafts itself. It is independent of the CIE Professional repository.

- **Alaya Crafts website:** the public home of the company, philosophy, landscape, products, organizational work, ideas, and contact path.
- **CIE Professional:** the first concrete product expression for individuals within the wider Alaya capability landscape.

CIE Professional informs the website's family resemblance—calmness, epistemic humility, progressive disclosure, paper/pine/amber visual language, and low cognitive friction—but does not own or define the corporate website.

## Current status

The approved V1 low-fidelity homepage study is preserved at [`design/studies/homepage-low-fidelity.html`](design/studies/homepage-low-fidelity.html). It remains a design artifact, not production code.

The study establishes the homepage narrative and responsive composition, the Capability Landscape, the explanation of Capability Intelligence and CIE, maturity asymmetry, the human Contact path, and section-level design rationale.

Future website UX, visual design, content, architecture decisions, implementation, and public assets belong in this repository.

## Technical foundation

The bootstrap is intentionally framework-free: semantic HTML, accessible responsive CSS, and restrained JavaScript.

No production framework has been selected because the approved study must first proceed through later design slices rather than being silently converted into implementation. This keeps the project lightweight and leaves the eventual production choice open until routing, content, build, and deployment needs are known.

## Current guardrails

- Orientation comes before conversion.
- The Capability Landscape is broader than Alaya's products and must not become a product architecture diagram.
- Capability Intelligence is the concept; CIE is the technology Alaya is building to make that understanding usable.
- CIE Professional is concrete today. Teams and Organizations represent other scales or directions. Collective Intelligence is a future possibility.
- Product maturity must remain visibly asymmetric.
- CIE must be explained before it is treated as a familiar acronym.
- Avoid generic service cards, unsupported claims, fake dashboards, fabricated proof, AI imagery, conversion pressure, and founder mythology.
- Contact remains direct and human; no chatbot or AI intermediary.
